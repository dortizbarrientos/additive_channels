How should each parent count?

Purple: parents below −1 SD for trait 1. Darker green bands mean higher log expected output; neighbouring bands differ by 0.5. The white ring is the starting mean; the diamond and triangle mark the curved-surface and plane-weighted means. A gold outline identifies the parent you selected.

What is being compared? The plane explains 90.2% of the variation in log expected reproductive output. The starting distribution gives each parent the same weight. Within either fitness model, a parent’s reproductive weight is its expected output divided by the sum across all 20,000 parents. The difference between the two weighted means is a difference in predicted selection within this population.
